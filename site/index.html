Architecture

MySQL is the transaction source of truth. A transactional outbox projects audit/read-model events into MongoDB. ethers.js is isolated behind a blockchain adapter.

Blockchain lifecycle

The on-chain anchor is broadcast without blocking on mining. A separate receipt-monitor worker reconciles the stored transaction hash.
